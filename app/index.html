    Help improve the readers

    The journal stays in this browser. It holds the command as the editor read it, with the names you typed replaced by a placeholder, what was understood, how sure the reader was, and what you did next. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you send it.

    What happens with a sent journal: the unplaced words, the unsure roles and the marked readings are grouped into gaps, a large model proposes new questions for the legend and new example sentences for each gap, the readers are retrained on the DGX and compared on the same frozen tests, and a person accepts or rejects each change before a new model ships. The process is described in the About panel.

    About this demo

    Every command is read in your browser by a VSS reader: a small model that turns a sentence into a frame of roles (what to do, to what, where), with a margin for every decision. Nothing is sent to a server, apart from the browser's own speech recognition when you use the microphone (see Settings).

    How the readers improve from use

    A command the editor does not understand is evidence that the legend lacks a question (a role), a word, or a formulation. Every reading carries that evidence: the words it could not place, the roles it was unsure about, the share of the sentence it could not explain, and whether you reformulated or marked it. With your permission the editor keeps this journal locally and you can send it. On our side the journal is grouped into gaps; a large model proposes, for each gap, a new question of the legend with example sentences, or new words and formulations; the proposals are validated mechanically (sop check), the readers are retrained on frozen data and compared with the previous ones on the same tests, and a person accepts each change before it ships. No model is ever changed directly: only the legend, the generators and the rules, all versioned.

    Build from a script

    Paste or type a document: a "# title", "## section" headings, one request per sentence. Every sentence is read; the diagram is built section by section, and a sentence the reader cannot read is marked, never guessed.
